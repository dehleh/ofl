3. The Opticom Approval Lifecycle (PRD Sections 6, 7 & 17)

The platform enforces Opticom's approved multi-tier governance matrix. Every credit case follows this strict sequential chain with role-segregated queues:

1
Application Intake & Identity Verification (Youverify Gateway)
Borrower or BDO submits facility request with 6-month bank statements, CAC certificate, and board resolution. Youverify automated API verifies BVN, NIN, RC status, and conducts PEP/Sanctions screening.
2
Credit Analyst Underwriting & Financial Spreading
Analyst evaluates verified bank turnover, spreads financials, executes deterministic ratio calculations (DSCR ≥ 1.30x, DSR ≤ 40%), validates collateral coverage (≥ 1.20x), and submits recommendation to HRM.
3
Head of Risk Management (HRM) — CAM Synthesis
HRM triggers Vertex AI Gemini to generate the 12-page official Credit Appraisal Memorandum (CAM). HRM reviews risk covenants, stress testing matrices, and routes the case to Head of Business Development.
4
Head of Business Development — Commercial Review & Sanction
Head of BD evaluates the commercial rationale, client relationship history, repayment dynamics, and interest margin. Upon commercial recommendation/approval, the case returns to HRM for final risk concurrence.
5
HRM Concurrence & Escalation to Final Executive Authority
HRM reviews BD's commercial recommendations. Upon risk concurrence, the engine checks the facility amount against Opticom's deterministic sanction thresholds:
Path A: Facilities ≤ ₦20,000,000 (MD Sanction)

Case routes to Managing Director (MD). MD grants final executive approval, automatically advancing case to Offer Pending and notifying Finance and Loan Operations.

Path B: Facilities > ₦20,000,000 (BCC Sanction)

MD reviews and endorses facility, escalating it to the Board Credit Committee (BCC). BCC Chairman and Committee members grant final board sanction, triggering offer generation.

6
Offer Letter Generation & Conditions Precedent Clearance
System generates formal facility Offer Letter with itemized Master Fee Schedule. Credit Operations verifies satisfaction of Conditions Precedent (e.g. registered mortgage, comprehensive insurance, promissory notes).
7
Treasury 1-Click LOAN-DISC / Xloandisk Booking & NAV Settlement
Treasury Lead executes 1-click disbursement: books loan into core LMS (LOAN-DISC / Xloandisk), generates NAV accounting voucher (e.g. NAV-DISB-XXXX), and logs SHA-256 audit entry.
