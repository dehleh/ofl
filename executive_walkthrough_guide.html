3. The Opticom Sequential Approval Chain & Exception Architecture

The platform enforces Opticom's approved multi-tier governance matrix. Every credit case follows this strict sequential chain with role-segregated queues and bi-directional exception rework routing:

1
Intake: Client Direct Portal OR BDO Assisted Intake
Prospective obligor applies via public portal or BDO submits request from field visit with bank statements, CAC certificate, board resolution, and collateral profile. Facility enters Submitted / Analyst Review.
2
Credit Analyst Underwriting & Exception Check
Analyst reviews financial statements, spreads turnover, and verifies documentation completeness.
• Exception / Incomplete Details: Analyst returns application to BDO Rework. BDO receives notification and opens case in assisted portal to supply missing items.
• Satisfied: Analyst leaves underwriting comments and forwards application to the Management Credit Committee (MCC).
3
MCC First Reviewer: Head of Business Development
The Head of Business Development is the first reviewer in MCC to receive the file from the Analyst.
• Deficiency / Query: Head of BD circles back to Credit Analyst with queries regarding missing applicant details.
• Satisfied: Head of BD records commercial comments and approves facility onward to the Head of Risk Management (HRM).
4
Head of Risk Management (HRM) — Risk Sanction & Alerting
HRM conducts comprehensive risk evaluation and covenants check (Vertex AI 12-page CAM synthesis).
• Risk Exception / Query: File routes back to Credit Analyst. Simultaneously, the Head of Business Development automatically receives an alert prompt notifying them of the exception.
• Satisfied: HRM logs risk sign-off and approves facility to the Managing Director (MD).
5
Managing Director (MD) — Executive Sanction & Auto Offer Dispatch
MD holds highest executive authority (≤ ₦20,000,000).
• Reservation / Query: MD returns file to either HRM (default) or Head of BD. Instant alert prompts are dispatched to Credit Analyst, HRM, and Head of BD.
• Executive Approval: MD grants final approval → Formal facility Offer Letter is automatically generated and instantly emailed to applicant with copy to Finance.

5. Continuous Loan Servicing & Default Recovery Platform

Following capital disbursement, the loan transitions automatically into Opticom's active telemetry platform. The engine performs continuous daily surveillance on repayment behavior, amortized collection status, and overdue obligations:

Real-Time Servicing Surveillance

Monitors monthly installment due dates, payments received, serviced principal vs interest, and remaining credit exposure across all active facilities.

Statutory 7-Day Grace Window

If an installment is missed on its due date, the borrower is granted a 7-calendar-day grace period during which status shows Grace Period Active and zero penalty is charged.

Deterministic 5% Default Penalty

On Day 8 past due date (grace expired), a deterministic 5% penalty is automatically levied on the unserviced monthly installment until paid.

6. Mathematical Formulation of the 5% Default Penalty Policy

Deterministic Default Penalty Policy Formula
Penalty Accrued = Monthly Installment × 5.0%   (Applied Deterministically on Day 8+)
Grace Condition: If Overdue Days ≤ 7 → Penalty = ₦0.00 • If Overdue Days > 7 → Penalty = 5% of monthly repayment.
