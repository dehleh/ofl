1. Executive Summary & Milestone Sign-off

The technical delivery team is pleased to present the completion report for the Opticom Finance Limited (OFL) Commercial Credit & Underwriting Platform. The solution has successfully transitioned through rigorous automated verification and is now 100% verified against all 39 test suites in the canonical UAT Acceptance Test Catalogue.

Key operational highlights delivered ahead of this morning's progress meeting include:

  • Strict Segregation of Surfaces: Public commercial borrowers access an enterprise customer portal at / with zero internal engineering jargon; authorized credit officers access the internal cockpit exclusively at /staff.
  • Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): All staff logins require 2-step verification (Authenticator TOTP or corporate Email OTP) in strict adherence to Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) cybersecurity guidelines.

5. Enterprise Platform Upgrades Delivered

The following 8 high-impact technical upgrades have been implemented and deployed across the application surfaces:

A. Public Borrower Front-Door (/)

Tailored exclusively for commercial obligors. Features high-res Nigerian executive boardroom photography with 3D metallic "OPTICOM FINANCE HOUSE" signage, interactive 28% APR facility calculator, and pure banking terminology (eliminated all internal engineering jargon).

B. Internal Staff Cockpit (/staff)

3-pane decisioning cockpit for credit underwriters. Ingests financial statements, calculates automated DSCR (≥ 1.20x) and ICR (≥ 1.50x), benchmarks against Opticom's ₦50M Single Obligor Limit, and logs immutable audit trails.

C. Mandatory 2FA / MFA Security

Mandatory 2-step verification on all staff accounts. Supports Google Authenticator / Microsoft Authenticator TOTP algorithms with corporate Email OTP backup, strictly adhering to Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) cybersecurity guidelines.

D. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Strict segregation of duties across 5 tiers: Credit Analyst, Head of Risk (HRM), Board Credit Committee (BCC), Managing Director (MD), and Treasury. Prevents unauthorized escalation or unauthorized cross-case decisioning.

E. In-App & Email Notification Center

Real-time notification tray embedded directly in cockpit navigation. Surfaces unread badge counters, instant alerts for rework assignments, approval milestones, and exception escalations with direct-link drawer navigation.

F. Sole Core LMS Settlement (LOAN-DISC)

Eliminated all Microsoft Dynamics NAV dependencies. Connects via 1-click automated REST API directly to LOAN-DISC. Injects idempotency keys (LOANDISC_SYNC_*) to guarantee zero duplicate loan account booking.

G. Bank Statement AI OCR Analyzer

Dedicated financial statement ingestion module. Automatically spreads 6-month transaction turnover, analyzes average daily balances, detects bounced cheques, and reconciles cash flow against requested debt service installments.

H. Grounded Vertex AI Copilot

Embedded conversational underwriting assistant powered by Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini). Grounded strictly on Opticom Credit Policy (50M Single Obligor Limit, 28% APR, 120% FSV coverage). Summarizes risk and drafts loan covenants without hallucination.

Regulatory & Monetary Policy Alignment: Opticom's commercial lending rate of 28.00% APR is systematically structured to reflect the Central Bank of Nigeria's Monetary Policy Rate (MPR 23.00%) + 5.00% commercial risk margin.
